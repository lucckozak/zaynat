Built for salons, not the other way around

Zaynat started from a simple observation: independent salons across the UAE were being asked to choose between a generic booking widget, a marketplace listing they didn't control, or expensive custom software. We think a salon should be able to get a real, branded booking presence online in minutes — and keep everything it earns.

What we believe

Three things we won't compromise on

Your own digital presence

A salon's booking site should belong to the salon — its own name, its own branding, its own web address. Not a listing buried in someone else's app.

No commission, ever

A flat monthly subscription, never a cut of your bookings. We're not planning to sit between a salon and its revenue.

No lock-in

Your data is yours. Export it, move it, or leave — a booking platform should earn a salon's business every month, not trap it.

Where we are

An early, working product

Zaynat is a young platform, built and improved in the open. Today it covers booking, calendar, staff and client management, and branding across UAE salons. Online payments, marketplace discovery, deeper analytics and more integrations are actively on the roadmap — see the Legal page for exactly what's live today versus what's coming.
