Features

Everything a salon needs to run online

Your own salon website

A branded booking site generated for every salon — logo, colours, gallery, services and team, live in minutes.

A calendar that can't double-book

Genuine per-specialist availability online; day/week/month view with buffers and working hours built in.

Staff & client management

Working hours, days off and commission for staff; history, notes and birthdays for clients.

Employee dashboard

Every specialist gets their own login — just their schedule, their appointments, their working hours and profile.

No-show protection
