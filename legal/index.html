Terms of Service

1. The service

Zaynat provides software that lets a salon create a branded booking website and manage staff, services, customers and appointments.

2. Accounts and roles

A salon owner registers an account and, in doing so, creates their salon on the platform. The owner controls which staff have access and to what. Zaynat maintains a separate platform-operator account used only to manage subscriptions and platform-wide settings — it does not grant access to a salon's customer data beyond what is needed to operate the subscription.

3. Payments

Subscription fees are billed by Zaynat to the salon.Zaynat does not currently process customer payments for bookings — there is no online payment or deposit collection in the booking flow today. When that is introduced, it will run through each salon's own connected account, never Zaynat's, and Zaynat does not take commission on bookings.

4. Suspension

A subscription that falls past due may be suspended: the salon's public booking site becomes unavailable and new bookings are disabled until the subscription is brought current. Suspension never deletes a salon's data — employees, customers, appointment history and settings remain intact and are restored on reactivation.

5. Cancellation

A salon owner may stop using Zaynat at any time. We intend to support exporting salon data on request before an account is closed.

6. Limitation of liability

The service is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind, to the fullest extent permitted by law. Zaynatis not liable for indirect or consequential losses arising from use of the platform.

7. Governing law

These terms are intended to be governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates, subject to a full legal review before the platform handles real customer data or payments.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

Salon owners provide business and contact details when registering; customers provide contact details when booking. Employee and customer records are entered by the salon itself.

Where it's stored

In the current build, all of this data — every salon's services, staff, customers and bookings — lives only in your own browser's local storage. Nothing is transmitted to a Zaynat server or any third party, because there is no server yet: this is a client-side prototype. A production deployment would move this to a proper database with encryption in transit and at rest, and this policy would be updated accordingly.

Tenant isolation

Each salon's data is kept in its own isolated storage slot. Staff and customers of one salon cannot see another salon's data through normal use of the product.

Cookies & local storage

Zaynat uses browser local storage to remember your session, your salon's data, and preferences like which salon is currently active in this tab. No advertising or analytics trackers are used in this build.

Your rights

You can request a copy of, or deletion of, your data by contacting the salon (for customer data) or Zaynat directly (for salon account data). Because storage is local to your browser today, clearing your browser's site data will also remove it.
